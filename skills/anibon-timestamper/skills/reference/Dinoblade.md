## Gameplay Systems

### Combat
- **Parry (ปัดป้อง / ปารรี่)** — core mechanic, must parry enemy attacks. Timing very strict. Comparable to Sekiro.
- **Block — hold to block, but parry is the intended mechanic.**
- **Parry timing:** stricter than Sekiro. Requires frame-perfect timing.
- **Unblockable red attacks — cannot parry, must dodge.**
- **Summon (อัญเชิญ)** — unlock after first boss. Summon spirits for bonus power.
- **Boss SP — special gauge, fill by parrying and attacking.**
- **Skill system — unlock skills for weapons and summons.**

### Equipment & Progression
- **Swords (ดาบ)** — weapons dropped by bosses. Upgradeable.
- **Earthshaker — boss sword reward from early boss.**
- **The Purple Resort — boss sword from Nosorus.**
- **Resonant Age — sword from late game area.**
- **Spirit summon — unlock spirits from bosses, each with unique effects.**
- **HP upgrade — increase health at checkpoints.**

### World
- **Dark forest (ป่ามืด)** — starting area.
- **Dinosaur field (ทุ่งหญ้าไดโนเสาร์)** — open grassland with roaming dinosaurs.
- **Mangrove forest (ป่าโกงกาง)** — swamp area.
- **Cursed king palace (ราชันคำสาป)** — late game castle area.
- **Flower field (ทุ่งดอกไม้)** — near end game story area.
- **Soulstones (ศิลาบันทึก)** — checkpoint system.

## Boat's Critical Review Points
- **Camera issues — very difficult to see attack patterns.** Main complaint.
- **Animation clarity — can't tell what the enemy is about to do.**
- **Difficulty vs. fairness — hard but doesn't feel well-designed.**
- **300 baht value — okay for the price, but not outstanding.**
- **Frame drops — performance issues, especially late game.**
- **Controller battery died mid-stream — switched to mouse+keyboard.**
- **Died 36 times total** before completing.
- **Parry window inconsistent — felt random at times.**
- **Comparisons to Sekiro (direct)** — Boat says Sekiro parry was more clear and fair.
- **Forgettable game overall** — fun for laughs, not a standout title.
