# Loop Hero — Knowledge Base

## 🔥 สถานะเกมและข้อมูลสรุป (Game Snapshot)
- **Title**: *Loop Hero* — Tactical roguelike deck-building & auto-battler RPG developed by **Four Quarters** and published by **Devolver Digital** (PC, Nintendo Switch).
- **Core Loop**: Hero auto-walks and fights along a looping path; player places terrain, roadside, and environment cards (`Meadow`, `Rock`/`Mountain`, `Forest`/`Thicket`, `River`, `Village`, `Cemetery`, `Vampire Mansion`, `Spider Cocoon`, `Battlefield`, `Goblin Camp`, `Treasury`, `Oblivion`, `Beacon`) to shape loot, resources, and stats, then fights Chapter Bosses before retreating to build Camp upgrades.
- **Playable Classes**: **Warrior** (balanced sword/shield, damage ramps per second), **Rogue** (dual-wielding, trophies instead of direct drops, Outpost support), **Necromancer** (summons skeletons, Magic HP shield).
- **Chapter Bosses (Acts 1–4)**: Act I **The Lich** (spawns Lich's Palace tiles), Act II **The Priestess** (Stained Glass shields & angels), Act III **The Hunter** (two hounds), Act IV **Omega** (Final Boss).
- **ASR Phonetic Traps**: `"หลูฮีโร่"` / `"Loopฮีโร่"` / `"ลูปฮีโร่"` → **Loop Hero**; `"สวิตช์ 2"` → **Nintendo Switch 2**; `"Baphomet"` / `"แวมไพร์"` → **Vampire** / **Vampire Mansion** / **Gargoyle**; `"โกบลิน"` / `"แคมกอบ"` → **Goblin Camp**.
