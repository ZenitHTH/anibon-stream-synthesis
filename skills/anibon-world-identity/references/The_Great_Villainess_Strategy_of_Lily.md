# The Great Villainess: Strategy of Lily (เผด็จการยูริ)

## Overview
- **Developer / Publisher**: Alliance Arts / WSS playground
- **Genre**: Turn-based Strategy / Tactical Yuri RPG
- **Platforms**: PC (Steam), Nintendo Switch
- **Community Thai Moniker**: "เผด็จการยูริ" (Dictator Yuri / Yuri Villainess)

## Lore & Core Mechanics
- **Protagonists**:
  - **Scarlett** (สการ์เล็ต): The villainess duchess sentenced to the guillotine who turns revolutionary dictator.
  - **Lily** (ลิลี่): The mysterious streaming girl who aids Scarlett with future technology and broadcasts.
- **Key Concepts**:
  - Streamer / Propaganda Broadcasts: Using public broadcasts to turn public opinion, demoralize enemy forces, and capture regions without bloodshed.
  - Turn-based Tactical Battles: Moving airships and commanding armies on hex/grid maps.
  - Collaborations: Official collaboration with *Touhou Project* (ZUN / คอลแลบเทพกินเหล้า).

## ASR Phonetic Traps & Invariants
- When Boat or chat refers to "เผด็จการยูริ" (Dictator Yuri), it is **The Great Villainess: Strategy of Lily**.
- Never confuse with *Command & Conquer: Red Alert 2 - Yuri's Revenge* unless explicit RTS/C&C context is mentioned.
