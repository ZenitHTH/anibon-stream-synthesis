## 🗡️ Modpack Overview

**Cisco's Fantasy Medieval RPG** is an extensive Minecraft (Java Edition) modpack transforming the sandbox experience into a Dark Fantasy Action RPG:
*   **Deep Magic & Class Progression:** Powered by Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks, featuring specialized magic schools, mana scaling, and custom spellcasters.
*   **Mythic Gear & Equipment Tiers:** Custom rarity tiers (Common through Mythic) with dynamic affixes, gems, and socketing mechanics.
*   **Formidable Bosses & Dragons:** World bosses featuring adaptive elemental resistances and lethal status debuffs (Bleed, Burn, Freeze).
*   **Special Mounts & Beasts:** Rideable creatures including **Lomar (โลมาร์)** for swift airborne exploration across perilous biomes.

---

## ⚙️ Key Mechanics, Crafting Tables & Controls

*   **Spell Wheel / Cycle (Keybind: R):** Hotkey allowing players to rotate equipped offensive and defensive spells mid-combat.
*   **Reforging Table & Salvaging Table (แท่นรีฟอร์จและย่อยไอเทม):** Core stations used to re-roll item stat affixes, modify sockets, and scrap unwanted rarity gear for enhancement shards.
*   **Forge Gem (เจมอัปเกรดเตาหลอม):** Socketable magical gems enhancing weapons, armor, and curios with passive attributes and elemental damage.
*   **Lomar (โลมาร์):** Agile mount used for high-speed terrain scouting and crossing dangerous lava canyons or chasms.
*   **Dragon Adaptive Resistance (การปรับตัวของมังกร):** Fire and Ice Dragons build dynamic defense against repeated damage types, forcing players to switch damage schools or employ strategic diversions (e.g., spawning a Wither to distract the dragon).
*   **Regeneration & Bleed:** High-level mob attributes that rapidly restore enemy HP and inflict damage-over-time status effects on the player.

## 🔮 Spells, Tomes & High-Tier Enchantments

- **Archmage Spellbook (หนังสือเวทจอมเวท):** High-tier grimoire offering expanded spell capacity, reduced cooldowns, and accelerated mana regeneration.
- **Gift of Origin / Shadow Gift (กิฟต์ออฟออริจิน / ชาโดว์กิฟต์):** Magical lineage talents unlocking void and dark magic affinities.
- **Shadow Grave (ชาโดว์เกรฟ):** Dark spell summoning a gravitational void vortex that pulls and damages surrounding enemies.
- **Perfect Silence (เพอร์เฟกต์ไซเลนซ์):** Disabling curse that suppresses enemy abilities, preventing counter-attacks and spellcasting.
- **Ice Breaker (ไอซ์เบรกเกอร์):** Cryo spell that shatters armor ratings and freezes targets in place.
- **Earth Quake (เอิร์ธเควก):** Geo spell unleashing shockwaves that stagger and deal massive AoE physical damage.
- **Endless Quiver (เอ็นด์เลสควิเวอร์):** Superior archery enchantment providing unlimited arrows with higher velocity and damage scaling than vanilla Infinity.
- **Splitting (สปลิตติง):** Archery enchantment causing arrows to fragment into multiple projectiles mid-air.
- **Fortune 5 & Unbreaking 5 (ฟอร์จูน 5 / อันเบรกกิง 5):** Over-leveled enchantment books obtained via specialized automated villager trading.

## 🏰 Entities, Mobs & Settlement Infrastructure

- **Allay (น้องอัลเลย์):** Helpful blue sprite mob imprisoned in Pillager Outposts that gathers and deposits matching items for the player.
- **Pillager & Outpost (พิลเลเจอร์และรังโจร):** Hostile illager towers and raiding parties; prime targets for looting high-tier Loot Crates.
- **Iron Golem (ไอรอนโกเล็ม):** Heavy iron defender spawned via villager panic mechanics, forming the backbone of automated iron farms.
- **Fire Dragon / Ice Dragon (มังกรไฟ / มังกรน้ำแข็ง):** Apex world bosses dropping dragon scales, bones, and crafting blood for Mythic dragon armors.
- **Auto Trader / Breeder Trader:** Automated settlement setups breeding villagers and cycling trade inventories for high-tier enchantments.
- **Smithing Template (สมิทธิงเทมเพลต):** Essential upgrade templates required for Netherite forging and modular equipment enhancements.

## 💬 Streamer Commentary & Insights (Pu Boat's Perspective)

*   **Solo vs. Multiplayer Experience:** Pu Boat strongly recommends Solo play for deep narrative immersion and challenging boss progression; multiplayer servers tend to trivialize difficult bosses through overwhelming player numbers.
*   **"Cisco's Modpack is Top-Tier":** Frequent praise for the balanced power progression, atmospheric medieval world design, and smooth integration of magic with melee combat.
*   **System Optimization & RAM Management:** Modpack runs with heavy shader packs; Pu Boat frequently monitors allocated RAM and adjusts render distance to prevent OBS encoder overload.
