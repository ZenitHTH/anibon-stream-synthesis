# 44th Golden Joystick Awards (November 11, 2026) — Knowledge Base

Reference for `anibon-world-identity` and `anibon-timestamper-local` when streams cover the **Golden Joystick Awards 2026** ceremony, winners, nominees, and related gaming news.

## 1. Ceremony Overview

- **Event**: 44th Annual Golden Joystick Awards (งานประกาศรางวัล Golden Joystick Awards ครั้งที่ 44)
- **Date & Venue**: November 11, 2026, at Troxy, London (11 พ.ย. 2026 ณ กรุงลอนดอน)
- **Host**: **Alix Wilton Regan (อลิกซ์ วิลตัน รีแกน)**
- **Top Winners**: **Resident Evil Requiem** (4 awards including Ultimate Game of the Year) and indie breakout **Meccha Chameleon** (4 awards)
- **Notable Eligibility Note**: **Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA VI)** was ineligible for the 2026 awards because its November 19, 2026 release falls after the late-October eligibility cutoff.
