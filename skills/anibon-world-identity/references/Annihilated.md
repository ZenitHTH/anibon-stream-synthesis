# Annihilated — Knowledge Base

## 🔥 สถานะเกมและข้อมูลสรุป (Game Snapshot)
- **Title**: *Annihilated* — Dark fantasy story-driven roguelite RPG developed by **Death Kumiai** and published by **Flashlight Games** (Steam, Nov 2026).
- **Premise**: Sole survivor of a rescue party enters a cursed dungeon to find and rescue one of four broken, fallen heroes (4 routes / 4 endings, turn-based combat + visual novel choices).
- **ASR Phonetic Traps**: `"anhilate"` / `"anate"` / `"unichilated"` / `"ไอแฟิบฮีโร่"` → **Annihilated**; `"broken Hero"` → **Broken Heroes**.
