## 3. Special Features & Convention Zones

- **สนามมวย TGS (Boxing Ring Booth)**: Interactive boxing ring inside the game show for creator matches, influencer show matches, and comedy brawls (Boat & Tod banter).
- **บอร์ดเกม & การ์ดเกม (Board Game & TCG Zone)**: Tabletop gaming zone featuring Thai board games ("บอร์ดเกมนรก", party games) and trading card games (One Piece Card Game, Pokémon TCG, Union Arena).
- **เวทีกลาง (Main Stage & Esports)**: Cosplay contests, esports tournaments, game publisher stage announcements, creator meet-and-greets.

## 4. Book Expo Thailand 2026 (Floor LG) Highlights

- **Major Manga & Light Novel Publishers**:
  - **Phoenix Next (ฟีนิกซ์ เน็กซ์)**: Kadokawa light novels, manga, acrylic stands, special boxes.
  - **Siam Inter Comics (สยามอินเตอร์)**: Shonen Jump manga, major weekly titles.
  - **DEX / Dexpress (เด็กซ์เปรส)**: Gundam, Ultraman, sci-fi/fantasy novels.
  - **First Page Pro (เฟิร์สเพจ โปร)**: Mature fantasy, isekai.
  - **Animag (อนิแม็ก)**: Re:Zero, Konosuba.
  - **Luckpim (รักพิมพ์)**: Rom-com, fantasy manga.
- **Key Subculture Concepts**:
  - *กองดอง (Tsundoku)*: Buying stacks of books to store unread.
  - *งบจำกัด*: Fixed-budget shopping runs.
