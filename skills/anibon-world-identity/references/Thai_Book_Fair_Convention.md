# Thai Book Fairs, Manga Publishers & Conventions Reference

Used by `anibon-world-identity` and `anibon-timestamper` to ensure correct terminology, publisher names, and culture tags when timestamping book fair walkthroughs, budget shopping challenges, and "ซื้อมาดอง" (Tsundoku) streams.

## 1. Major Book Fairs & Venues in Thailand

| Event | Thai Name | Venue | Characteristics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **National Book Fair** | สัปดาห์หนังสือแห่งชาติ | Queen Sirikit National Convention Center (QSNCC) / Bang Sue | Held in March–April, biggest annual book fair in Thailand |
| **Book Expo Thailand** | มหกรรมหนังสือแห่งชาติ | Queen Sirikit National Convention Center (QSNCC) / IMPACT | Held in October, massive manga, novel, and general literature expo |
| **Comic Avenue / Comic Square** | คอมมิค อเวนิว / CA | Various convention halls | Independent doujinshi & creator book fair |

## 3. Book Fair Subculture & Streamer Terminology

- **ซื้อมาดอง / กองดอง (Tsundoku)**: The cultural practice of buying piles of manga/novels with the intention of reading them later (often left piled up unread). A recurring comedic trope in ANIBON streams.
- **งบจำกัด (Budget Challenge)**: Shopping challenges where the streamer and friends set a fixed cash limit (e.g. 1,000 THB) to see who buys the best value bundle.
- **ของพรีเมียม / ของแถม (Premiums & Freebies)**: Promotional items given for minimum spending or buying special edition sets (clear files, postcards, acrylic stands, bookmarks).
- **เล่มจบ / Boxset**: Complete series bundles or commemorative collector boxes.
- **การ์ดสะสม / บอร์ดเกม (Trading Cards & Board Games)**: Tabletop gaming booths (Warhammer 40K, Magic: The Gathering, One Piece Card Game) frequently present in Book Fair halls.
- **บูธสำนักพิมพ์ / ต่อแถว (Publisher Booths & Queueing)**: Standing in line at high-demand booths (Phoenix, First Page, Luckpim) to grab limited editions before they sell out.
