## 👾 Key Entities, Mobs & Bosses

- **Ender Dragon (มังกรดิเอ็นด์):** Primary flying world boss of The End; players must neutralize healing End Crystals atop obsidian pillars before taking it down.
- **Wither (วิทเธอร์):** Player-summoned undead boss crafted from 4 Soul Sand blocks and 3 Wither Skeleton skulls; drops Nether Stars required for Beacons.
- **Warden (วาร์เดน):** The most devastating mob in vanilla Minecraft; blind apex predator that detects players strictly through sonic vibrations and scent.
- **Breeze (บรีซ):** Agile wind entity guarding Trial Chambers that unleashes bouncing Wind Charges to stagger players and trigger environmental traps.
- **Allay (น้องอัลเลย์):** Benevolent blue flying companion rescued from Pillager Outposts; collects dropped ground items matching whatever object it holds.
- **Villager (ชาวบ้าน):** Trade-based NPCs capable of acquiring professions, providing enchanted books (Mending, Unbreaking), and driving automated crop/iron farms.
- **Pillager & Ravager (พิลเลเจอร์และราเวเจอร์):** Aggressive illager raiders wielding crossbows and heavy war-beasts; assault player settlements during village Raids.
- **Iron Golem (ไอรอนโกเล็ม):** Sturdy mechanical guardian built from iron blocks or summoned naturally via villager panic; central to automated iron farming.
- **Bogged (บ็อกด์):** Moss-covered skeleton variant native to swamps and Trial Chambers that fires poison-tipped arrows.
- **Armadillo (อาร์มาดิลโล):** Passive savannah and badlands mob that curls into a defensive shell when threatened; sheds Armadillo Scutes for Wolf Armor crafting.
- **Piglin & Piglin Brute (พิกซินและพิกซินบรูท):** Nether dwellers pacified only by gold equipment; Piglin Brutes guard Bastion Remnants and attack relentlessly regardless of gold.

## ⚔️ High-Tier Gear, Artifacts & Core Mechanics

- **Mace (กระบองเมซ):** Devastating heavy blunt weapon crafted from a Heavy Core and Breeze Rod; multiplies damage exponentially based on downward falling velocity.
- **Smithing Template & Armor Trim (สมิทธิงเทมเพลตและลายชุดเกราะ):** Forging patterns discovered in world structures used to upgrade Diamond equipment to Netherite and apply decorative trims.
- **Netherite (เนเธอไรต์):** Indestructible alloy immune to lava incineration; crafted from ancient debris into ingots for upgrading top-tier weapons and armor.
- **Ominous Bottle & Ominous Trial:** Consumable potion triggering bad omen status, converting standard Trial Chamber spawners into high-difficulty Ominous Trials with exclusive rewards.
- **Redstone Automation (วงจรเรดสโตน):** Complex electrical and logic system enabling fully automated item sorters, flying machines, mob farms, and piston contraptions.

## 🛠️ The Minecraft Modding Community & CurseForge Ecosystem

**CurseForge** serves as the primary distribution platform and central hub for Minecraft community modding, hosting thousands of individual mods, curated modpacks, and shader packs.

### 1. Modern Mod Loaders
*   **NeoForge:** The modern, community-led fork of Minecraft Forge; provides superior stability, modern event hooks, and optimal compatibility for 1.20+ and modern drops.
*   **Minecraft Forge:** The historic standard loader responsible for the golden age of tech, industrial, and large-scale kitchen-sink modpacks.
*   **Fabric:** Lightweight and highly optimized mod loader renowned for rapid updates and high-performance client/server modifications.
*   **Quilt:** Open-source modular fork of Fabric emphasizing expanded developer tooling and broad compatibility.

### 2. Major Modpack Categories & Signature Titles
*   **RPG, Dark Fantasy & Hardcore Survival:**
    *   **Cisco's Fantasy Medieval RPG:** Dark fantasy overhaul featuring custom classes, Iron's Spells magic, boss scaling, and rideable Lomars.
    *   **RLCraft:** Notorious hardcore survival pack integrating thirst, body-temperature regulation, and brutal Lycanites Mobs hazards.
    *   **DawnCraft & Prominence II:** Souls-like action RPG packs incorporating custom combat animations, dodge rolls, and rich narrative questlines.
*   **Technology, Machinery & Industrial Automation:**
    *   **Create (เครียต):** Aesthetic kinetic engineering mod featuring rotating shafts, cogwheels, conveyor belts, and realistic train networks.
    *   **Applied Energistics 2 (AE2) & Refined Storage:** Digital item storage networks utilizing ME drives, spatial storage, and automated molecular crafting.
    *   **Mekanism & Thermal Expansion:** High-efficiency ore multiplication chains, chemical processing plants, and nuclear reactors.
*   **Magic, Witchcraft & Spellcraft:**
    *   **Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks:** Modular spellcasting mod adding 10 magic affinities, wizard gear, and spell scroll crafting (core engine of Cisco's RPG).
    *   **Ars Nouveau & Botania:** Flexible custom spell creation through glyphs, alongside nature-based floral mana automation.
*   **Dungeons, Dimensions & World Boss Overhauls:**
    *   **L_Ender 's Cataclysm:** High-octane dungeon boss mod introducing fearsome foes like Ignis, the Leviathan, and Netherite Monstrosity.
    *   **Mowzie's Mobs:** High-polish fantasy creatures with unique AI patterns and telegraphed combat mechanics.
    *   **The Twilight Forest & The Aether:** Legendary classic dimension mods offering enchanted evergreen forests and heavenly floating skylands.
*   **Performance, Shaders & Engine Optimization:**
    *   **Sodium / Embeddium:** Ground-up modern OpenGL rendering engines delivering massive FPS gains on modern hardware.
    *   **Iris Shaders / Oculus:** Cutting-edge shader loader allowing real-time path-traced lighting and dynamic shadows without performance penalties.
    *   **FerriteCore & MemoryLeakFix:** Crucial RAM optimization mods minimizing memory footprints to resolve streamer encoder overload issues.
*   **Essential Utility Mods:**
    *   **JEI (Just Enough Items) / EMI / REI:** Indispensable on-screen crafting recipe viewer and ingredient lookup catalog.
    *   **Jade / WTHIT:** Real-time HUD tooltip displaying block names, chest contents, mob HP, and active potion effects.
    *   **JourneyMap / Xaero's World Map & Minimap:** Full-featured waypoint navigation and real-time radar mapping.
