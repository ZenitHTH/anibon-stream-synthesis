## 3. Valmiki Ramayana, Hindu Lore & Thai Ramakien (รามเกียรติ์) Comparisons

| English Name (Thai Name) | Role / Tradition | Attribute / Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Valmiki (วาลมิกิ)** | Author / Sage | Ancient Sanskrit poet who composed the original *Valmiki Ramayana* |
| **Ramakien (รามเกียรติ์)** | Thai National Epic | Thai adaptation composed under King Rama I (รัชกาลที่ 1); localizes characters for Thai court & Khon |
| **Ramesh Menon (รเมศ เมนอน)** | Author | Writer of accessible modern English/Thai-translated *Ramayana* prose retelling |
| **Trimurti (ตรีมูรติ)** | Hindu Triad | Brahma (พระพรหม — Creator), Vishnu (พระวิษณุ/พระนารายณ์ — Preserver/Balance), Shiva (พระศิวะ/พระอิศวร — Destroyer) |
| **Dashavatara (ทศาวตาร / นารายณ์ 10 ปาง)** | Avatars of Vishnu | Ten incarnations of Vishnu (e.g., Parashurama 6th, Rama 7th, Krishna 8th); Thai literary work by King Rama VI (ร.6) |
| **Vishnu / Narayana (พระวิษณุ / พระนารายณ์)** | Supreme Preserver | Reincarnated as human Prince Rama to defeat Ravana under Brahma's boon |
| **Lakshmi (พระแม่ลักษมี)** | Goddess | Consort of Vishnu; incarnated as Sita |
| **Parashurama (ปรศุราม)** | 6th Avatar of Vishnu | Axe-wielding Brahmin warrior, disciple of Shiva, enraged when Rama breaks Shiva's Pinaka bow |
| **Vishwamitra (วิศวามิตร)** | Royal Sage | Mentors young Rama and Lakshmana against Tataka and into Mithila |
| **Jaya and Vijaya (ชัย และ วิชัย)** | Vaikuntha Gatekeepers | Vishnu's dwarapala attendants cursed by the Four Kumaras to be reborn as demonic adversaries (culminating in Ravana/Kumbhakarna) |
| **Rakshasa (รากษส)** | Race / Beings | Powerful non-human tribe in Valmiki Ramayana (distinct from Thai "ยักษ์" / giant ogre depiction) |
| **Ravana / Thotsakan (ราวัณ / ทศกัณฐ์)** | King of Lanka | Ten-headed scholar/ascetic who earns Brahma's boon and receives his name ("He who roars") under Mount Kailash |
| **Bharata (พระพรต)** | Prince of Ayodhya | Rama's virtuous half-brother who refuses the throne and rules with Rama's sandals |
| **Shatrughna (พระสัตรุฆน์)** | Prince of Ayodhya | Lakshmana's twin brother |
| **Vibhishana / Phiphek (วิภีษณะ / พิเภก)** | Ravana's Brother | Righteous brother/astrologer who defects to Rama |
| **Kumbhakarna (กุมภกรรณ)** | Ravana's Brother | Giant warrior cursed to sleep for months |
| **Jambavan (ชามพวาน / ชมพูพาน)** | King of Bears | Wise bear warrior in Valmiki's Vanara army (portrayed as a monkey in Thai *Ramakien*) |
| **Vali / Phali (พาลี)** | Vanara King | Elder brother of Sugriva; cursed/boon to take half an opponent's strength |
| **Sugriva / Sukhrip (สุครีพ)** | Vanara King | Younger Vanara brother who allies with Rama |
| **Pushpaka Vimana (บุษบกวิมาน)** | Flying Chariot | Ravana's celestial flying chariot seized from Kubera |
| **Swayamvara (สยุมพร)** | Royal Ceremony | Contest where Rama strings and breaks Shiva's bow to wed Sita |
| **Bala Kanda (พาลกัณฑ์)** | Book 1 | Rama's birth, training under Vishwamitra, and marriage to Sita |
| **Ayodhya Kanda (อโยธยากัณฑ์)** | Book 2 | Kaikeyi's boons and Rama's 14-year exile from Ayodhya |
| **Aranya Kanda (อรัณยกัณฑ์)** | Book 3 | Forest exile in Panchavati, Surpanakha, golden deer, Jatayu, Sita's abduction |
| **Khon (โขน)** | Thai Classical Dance | UNESCO-recognized Thai masked dance-drama performing *Ramakien* |
